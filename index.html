What is Oplan Kontra Baha?

Short-term program

Oplan Kontra Baha (OKB) is a short-term government program.

“Quick Win”

Objective

To make sure floodwater has a clear path to flow.

Examples

Clearing of garbage

that clogs canals and drainage

Removal of obstructions

in waterways, especially illegal structures

Dredging of rivers

deepening or widening of rivers

Pumping stations

installing new ones or repairing broken ones
